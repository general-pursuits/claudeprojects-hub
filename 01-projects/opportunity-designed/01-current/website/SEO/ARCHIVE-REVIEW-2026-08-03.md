# Archive Review — What's Worth Bringing Back
*Reviewed August 3, 2026: version-archive (FULLv5 historical site copy, Removed Homepage Copy, Master Brand Handbook), 02-archive (old About, pre-sync Playbook, brand voice guidelines, old strategy-studio site), and the July 29 SEO competitive landscape — all checked line by line against the current live site, field notes, and llms.txt.*

## The headline finding

Your own strongest positioning language got trimmed out of the site over the July clarity passes, and today you said it back to me almost word for word. The old site said "recognizing **unrealized potential**, envisioning what does not yet exist, and **architecting the path** required to bring it to life." That is exactly the "I find unrealized opportunities / I architect opportunity" framing you gave me this morning for the Blueprint closer — and the word "unrealized" appears nowhere on the live site today. The archive confirms this is your native vocabulary, not a new idea. Recommendation: work "unrealized" into the Blueprint closer and llms.txt (one word each), e.g. "architects the **unrealized** opportunity around it."

## Terminology worth resurrecting (absent from the live site today)

**Philosophy lines that are pure Addie and currently unused:**
- "**Growth Follows Differentiation.**" — from the Handbook. One of your three founding principles; the other two ("See What Others Miss", "Opportunity Doesn't Appear. It's Built.") are live. This one fits the Blueprint's Design step or the glossary as a named principle.
- "**Operator first, consultant second.**" — from the old About. Your best one-line credential; nothing on the live site says it this compactly. Candidate for the About founder section and every bio (LinkedIn, Qwoted, Crunchbase).
- "A sharp outside read **without the McKinsey bill**" — old About. Punchy, maybe too spicy for the site, but excellent for LinkedIn posts.
- "A Different Perspective Creates Different Outcomes." — old founder headline; good section header or LinkedIn banner line.

**Service vocabulary buyers actually search, all missing from the live site:** assortment strategy (one field-note mention only), **MAP / pricing policy**, vendor negotiation, merchandising operations, brand roster design, open-to-buy, independent retailer, specialty outdoor, direct-to-retail. These were the old "What We Do" list. The current site describes the work narratively but never names these services — which is why Google can't rank you for them. Cheapest fix: a services-vocabulary sentence on the homepage or About, and glossary entries for open-to-buy and brand roster.

**Credentials that vanished:** the old About named **Senior Buyer at Backcountry** (running footwear across Nike, Salomon, Norda, Brooks…) and **Director of Purchasing at New World Natural Brands**. The live site references the experience but never states the titles. Titles are what E-E-A-T and AI engines cite. Recommendation: restore both titles verbatim to the About founder section, LinkedIn, and llms.txt.

## What NOT to bring back

The old three-phase framework names (Opportunity Mapping → Tactical Innovation → Growth Execution) are superseded by The Opportunity Blueprint — resurrecting them would fork the vocabulary you're trying to consolidate. And the removed "You receive:" deliverable lines conflict with today's ruling that outputs vary by client and framework copy never promises a fixed deliverable format; leave them archived for proposals only, where scope is real.

## Suggested order

Free and immediate: unrealized + titles + "Operator first, consultant second" into the About edit already in flight, service-vocabulary sentence, Costco H2, Innerview mention, fractional sentence. Then the MAP pricing page (biggest prize), then merchandising-consultant page, then the pillar page.
