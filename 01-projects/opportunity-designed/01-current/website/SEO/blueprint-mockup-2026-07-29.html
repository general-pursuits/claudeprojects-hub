The Method
The Opportunity Blueprint

Ten years on the buying side of the table, turned into a method. Every engagement runs the same sequence, whatever the size of the brand.

01

Map

Where is the opening? Category structure, shelf reality, the margin math, the whitespace competitors stopped watching.

02

Design

What is the blueprint? Positioning, price architecture, assortment, and the retail path: which doors, in what order, with what story for the buyer. Designed so a buyer can say yes to it.

03

Build

What makes it stick? Buying and merchandising set up inside the business, holding full price season after season, until the niche is unmistakably the brand's own. Made to outlast the engagement.

Under every step sits operational discipline: systems tuned to run lean, built to be handed off, made to last for your team and grow with your business. For brands that want it, that can include AI, applied with operator judgment where it makes the work better. For those that don't, the systems run just as well without it.
The Blueprint starts from the brand's core and architects the unrealized opportunity around it: a niche of the brand's own, built so the client leads the space and others have to follow. Growth follows differentiation. It is the method behind performance premium, premium goods selling at full price, at volume.
Every engagement starts somewhere on the Blueprint and takes only the steps the business needs.
