## 3. Reading the retargeted dashboard

- **Leading indicators (weeks):** your domain appears in the *citations* column for any tracked prompt; mentions move 0 → nonzero.
- **Lagging (months):** share of voice and sentiment vs the boutique set.
- **Feedback loop:** any prompt where a competitor is cited and you aren't = the brief for your next guide or FAQ entry. That's the compounding loop — the audit is a content-request queue once it's pointed at the right market.
