### Post 04 - Thu 2026-10-08 - The Opportunity Blueprint
Image: post-04-blueprint-od-2026-09-24.png

Every engagement follows the same path. We call it The Opportunity Blueprint.

Map. Where is the opening? Category structure, search behavior, margin math, and competitive gaps.

Design. What is the blueprint? Positioning, price structure, product selection, and the retail entry sequence.

Build. What makes it stick? Buying and merchandising operations that hold full price and keep the lead.

The scope changes with each brand. The path stays the same.
