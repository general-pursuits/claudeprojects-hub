00A Different Perspective Creates Different Outcomes.
Fig. 01Salt Lake City, UT
Addie MorrowIndependent Business Consultant

Operator first, consultant second. I spent a decade in-house as a retail buyer, most recently Senior Buyer at Backcountry and before that Director of Purchasing at New World Natural Brands, buying and merchandising across 35+ consumer brands.

Over the last decade I've worked across retail buying, merchandising, operations, commercial strategy, and vendor partnerships, reading shifts in consumer behavior before they're obvious and turning them into growth.

The most valuable opportunities rarely fit a proven playbook. New territory reads as risk, the moment passes, and the market moves on. That unfamiliar space is where growth lives, and it's why I founded Opportunity Designed.

My approach makes it navigable. I break down the complexity, build on what you do well, and move you toward market space where you set the pace instead of following. There's no standard formula. You invent your own, and that's what I help you do.

I work alongside your team, crossing the finish line together with a strategy you can actually run, one that favors resourcefulness over big budgets. You come away with what matters: a uniquely competitive position and a team ready to run it.

In practice, that work is named plainly: assortment strategy, MAP and pricing policy, vendor negotiation planning, merchandising operations, and brand roster design, delivered as a defined project or as fractional retail buying and merchandising leadership.

Common questions

Asked & answered

01What does a growth strategy consultant do for a consumer brand?

I find the growth opportunities a brand can't see from the inside, then build the plan to capture them: assortment and merchandising strategy, retail and marketplace expansion, and the operational path to execute. The work draws on ten years in-house at major retailers, so the plan reflects how buyers actually make decisions.

02How do engagements work?

Every engagement is shaped around the business, from an Opportunity Review to a defined growth project, a full operational buildout, or ongoing advisory. The structure comes out of a conversation: it starts with a 20-minute Project Fit Call, not a contract.

03Who do you work with?

Consumer brands that are ready to grow, and the retailers and marketplaces that sell them. Past work spans outdoor, footwear, beauty, wellness, and Amazon-native brands, with placements into Ulta, Costco, Target, and Whole Foods.

04Where are you based, and do you work remotely?

Opportunity Designed is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, and works with brands across the US. Most engagements run remotely, with on-site time where the work calls for it.

05What makes your approach different from a typical consultant?

Ten years on the buying side means the recommendations reflect how a retailer actually makes a yes-or-no decision, not just how a brand would like to be perceived. The plan accounts for what a buyer is weighing on their side of the table, because I’ve sat there.

06Do you take on ongoing work, or only single projects?

Both. Some clients need a defined project with a clear end point; others want an ongoing advisory relationship as the business keeps growing. Which one fits usually becomes clear during the Project Fit Call, not before it.
