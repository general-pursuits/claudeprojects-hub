Common questions

Project Fit Call FAQ

01What is a Project Fit Call?

A 20-minute video call about where your brand or category stands, what is getting in the way, and whether working together makes sense. It is a conversation, not a sales pitch.

02Is there a cost for the call?

No. There is no charge for the Project Fit Call. Paid work is scoped afterward, and only if there is a fit.

03Who will I talk with?

Addie Morrow, Founding Consultant of Opportunity Designed, with 10+ years in retail buying and merchandising across 35+ brands.

04How should I prepare?

Come ready to share what you sell, who you sell to or for, and where growth stalls. No deck or materials are needed.

05Where does the call happen?

On Google Meet. The link is in the calendar invite and confirmation email you receive after booking.

06What happens after the call?

If there is a fit, the next step is a scope for the work, such as an Opportunity Review, a defined growth project, or ongoing advisory. If not, you still leave with a clearer next step.

07Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Use the reschedule or cancel link in your confirmation email to pick a new time.
