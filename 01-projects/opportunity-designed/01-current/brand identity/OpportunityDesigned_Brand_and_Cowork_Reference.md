## Color system (exact, from the Brand Quick-Reference plus live CSS)

Ground rule: never true black or true white. Violet is the single accent note, not a fill.

**Standard combinations.** Dark section: charcoal bg, off-white `#EDEAE3` text, violet accent, lavender eyebrows, hover deep violet. Light section: parchment bg, near-black `#1A1714` text, violet accent, lavender eyebrows, hover deep violet.

## Typography

Two typefaces only, both free on Google Fonts.

- **Display / Headlines:** Anton, weight 400, always uppercase. Condensed, industrial. Used for H1s, section numerals, and every headline.
- **Body / UI:** Inter, weights 300 to 700. 300 for long-form body; 500 to 700 for labels, buttons, emphasis; 600 semibold with wide letter-spacing for eyebrows and section labels.
- Use all-caps or lowercase; avoid default Title Case. White space reads as authority.

## Logo mark

Stipple-gradient stamp mark, inspired by a hand-pressed hanko. Not a bubbly cloud with outlined lobes: a fingerprint or thumb-swipe shape where the stipple density itself creates the gradient (dense center fading to lone scattered dots), no outlines or rim. Smooth elongated ellipse about 4.8:1, slightly tilted and asymmetric.

**Mark meaning (verbatim from the site):** a cloud (see what others miss) plus an inkblot (value what's overlooked) plus a fingerprint (own what's unmistakably yours).

Master set in `01-current/mark/`: black, obsidian, violet, silver, lavender, ochre, parchment, and white variants, plus a vector master `mark-cleaned-vector.svg` and favicon/app icons at 32, 64, 180, 512px. Source `source-mark-black.png` is RGBA with the stipple in the alpha channel (recolor by replacing RGB, keeping alpha).

**Quick picks:** LinkedIn profile pic = `icon-512-black-on-white.png`; white deck = `mark-black-transparent.png`; dark deck = `mark-white-transparent.png`; email signature = `mark-black-transparent.png`; social light bg = `mark-violet-on-parchment.png`; social dark bg = `mark-violet-on-obsidian.png`.

## Photography and art direction

- Never posed: always mid-motion, from behind, or intimate detail.
- Grainy, unfiltered, earned: think mud-caked shoes worn with pride.
- Light versus dark, not styling: drama comes from contrast, not art direction.
- No safe middle ground: industrial space or open wilderness. Salt Lake City and the Wasatch are fair game.
- Visual reference brands (visual only, not voice): Satisfy Running, Berghain, gnuhr_studio, milerrunning.

## Voice rules

- **Register:** warm, confident, benefit-led, first person, plain sentences with one idea each. One word: "Clear." Contractions are fine.
- **Banned words:** "gut check," "honest" / "honestly," absolute "never" (use conditional language; industry terms like "never-out-of-stock replenishment" are fine), "free" (the call is "a 20-minute diagnostic call"), "playbook" (rejected in the Norda case study).
- **Banned punctuation:** em dashes anywhere in OD materials. Rewrite with periods, colons, or commas.
- **Avoid:** buzzwordy or AI-sounding copy, triple-item flourishes, wordy compound sentences. Scan everything before delivery.

## Copy style

Easy to understand yet impactful in the fewest words and characters. Anchor to the tagline and the core lines. Keep branding streamlined and consistent across every channel and document.

# PART B · OPERATING RULES

- **Deliverables, not hours.** Never commit days per week or hours per month. Price on scope and outcomes. Tier axes: scope depth, deliverables, working sessions included.
- **No confidential performance metrics in public.** Never publish sell-through, revenue, margin, unit, or growth figures (client or employer data, legal exposure). Prove with facts, not figures: firsts, durations, public counts, placements, qualitative outcomes.
- **SEO plus LLM/AI search, heavy emphasis.** Best-practice SEO on the site and all content including images (alt text plus filenames). Emphasize AI-search: question and answer framing, definitional content, entity clarity and cross-linking, citable structured facts. Audit and improve proactively.
- **Always show mockups, mobile and web, mobile-first.** A reviewable mockup before finalizing anything visual; both views; design mobile-first.
- **Free plans always.** Every tool on its free tier (Cal.com, Zapier, HubSpot, Netlify, and any new tool). Flag paid features and offer the free route first. Zapier: 2-step Zaps only, 100 tasks/month. Netlify: batch deploys, approval-first.
- **AI model cost tiering.** Cheaper models for simple tasks; reserve stronger models for complex work.
- **Docs synced on Mac and Notion.** Local is source of truth; Notion mirrors it; keep both in sync.
- **Email channels.** adelitamorrow@gmail.com is never business-facing (login only). addie@opportunitydesigned.com is the personal channel (lead replies, proposals, signature). hello@opportunitydesigned.com is the front-door channel (public contact, generic inbound, company-voice sends).
- **Website standing rules.** Any copy change needs approval first as a labeled mockup; one deploy per approval round. Bump the `?v=` cache stamp on every deploy that touches CSS/JS/fonts/images. Archive removed content word-for-word, never delete.
