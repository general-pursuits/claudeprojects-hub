## Target 1: Bombas

ZoomInfo company id 357506573. NYC. $237M revenue, 157 employees, private, B Corp.

### Why now
- May 2025: Jason LaRose named CEO, ex-Under Armour President North America. Press framed
  the hire as scaling beyond DTC roots.
- July 2025: $96.7M raise.
- Nov 2025: third physical store opened in Austin via Leap, Simon and Shopify.
- Aug 2026: more open marketing roles than at any point in the prior six months.

### The gap
Enterprise retail leadership and fresh capital, but no visible wholesale door strategy.
Four categories (socks, underwear, tees, slippers) with deep SKU counts and no simplified
retail assortment. The one-for-one donation story sells DTC and complicates wholesale
margin and shelf-space conversations.

### Angle
Assortment architecture and wholesale positioning for retail buyers: a simplified,
planogram-ready line built for buyer sell-through, and repositioning of the brand story
for a wholesale negotiation rather than a consumer one.
