# The Baddie Pack

Project home for building and operating a hyperlocal dog-walking business serving Westgate, Paperbox, Zephyr, and nearby Downtown Salt Lake City residences.

## Current concept

- **Business:** The Baddie Pack
- **Core service:** Baddie Walkies
- **Recurring-client program:** The Wag List
- **Photo and social feature:** Hawt Dog Paparazzi
- **Milestone rewards:** Baddie Charms
- **Primary line:** Downtown dog walking for people with places to be.
- **Campaign line:** Don’t have that dog in you today? Your dog still does. We’ll take it from here.

## Launch principle

Start as a focused neighborhood walking service. Do not add sitting, group adventures, retail inventory, or complicated memberships until the core walking workflow is safe, repeatable, and generating demand.

## Immediate next decision

Define the initial service durations, service radius, operating hours, and introductory pricing after local competitor and cost research.
