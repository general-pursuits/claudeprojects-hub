# Service Model

This is a draft framework. Durations, prices, hours, capacity, and cancellation terms are not yet approved.

## Launch services

### Baddie Walkies — quick relief

Short neighborhood outing for bathroom relief and light movement. Final duration and suitability rules are TBD.

### Baddie Walkies — standard walk

The intended core service: a neighborhood walk, fresh water check, basic cleanup, and Walk Report.

### Baddie Walkies — extended walk

Longer movement and enrichment for dogs suited to the duration, weather, and route.

### Drop-in visit

At-home visit for bathroom relief, water, feeding as instructed, medication only if explicitly accepted and safely within scope, and brief companionship. Medication services require a separate written policy before launch.

## The Wag List

Potential recurring-client benefits:

- Reserved recurring time windows
- Priority access to newly opened availability
- Milestone Baddie Charms
- Seasonal bandanas when safely designed and economically justified

Do not advertise this as a membership until its price, benefits, cancellation rules, and capacity limits are documented.

## Not offered at initial launch

- Off-leash walks
- Unsupervised dog-park visits
- Group walks with unfamiliar dogs
- Boarding or overnight care
- Dog transportation
- Training or behavioral treatment
- Veterinary or medical services
- Retail jewelry worn on collars without a documented safety standard

These boundaries can change only after risk, insurance, capability, and demand are reviewed.

## Every completed service should include

- Arrival and departure confirmation
- Leash, harness, and visible-condition check
- Service delivered within the agreed window
- Water check when access is provided
- Waste pickup and appropriate disposal
- Walk Report with bathroom notes and relevant observations
- At least one photo when safe and permitted
- Prompt disclosure of anything unusual
