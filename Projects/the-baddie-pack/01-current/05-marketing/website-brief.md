# Website Brief

## Website objective

Convert nearby dog owners into qualified meet-and-greet requests while demonstrating that the service is local, funny, discreet, and reliable.

## Proposed URL

Preferred: `thebaddiepack.com`

Do not purchase until domain, Utah business-name, social-handle, and trademark checks are complete. A temporary hosted preview can be used during development.

## Recommended pages

### Home

- Clear service and location
- Primary value proposition
- Trust indicators
- Core services
- How it works
- Meet-and-greet call to action

### Services

- Duration and scope
- Pricing
- What is included
- Eligibility and boundaries
- Cancellation and access fees

### About Addie

- Relevant experience and care philosophy
- Clear local connection
- Background-check, insurance, and bonding status only when verified
- Friendly photo that matches the service clients will actually receive

### Safety and trust

- Meet-and-greet requirement
- Access and privacy practices
- Photo-consent choices
- Emergency approach
- Insurance/background-check verification when completed

### Book

- Fit-request or approved-client booking flow
- Service-area confirmation
- No exposure of personal calendar information

### Policies and contact

- Cancellation, weather, behavior, payment, privacy, and emergency boundaries
- `hello@thebaddiepack.com` once the domain is purchased

## Current booking-page copy

### Heading

**The Baddie Pack**

### Descriptor

Reliable neighborhood dog walking for Westgate, Paperbox, Zephyr, and surrounding Downtown SLC residences.

### Supporting copy

Every walk includes attentive care, a judgment-free talk-therapy session, and photo evidence that your dog is, in fact, more popular at bunco night than you are.

### Call to action

**Request a meet and greet**

This is safer than presenting every visitor with instant unscreened access to a home-service booking.

## Search language to use naturally

- Downtown Salt Lake City dog walker
- Downtown SLC dog walking
- Dog walking near Westgate, Paperbox, and Zephyr
- Recurring neighborhood dog walks

Do not stuff these phrases into every paragraph.
