# Brand and Positioning

## Brand architecture

### The Baddie Pack

The umbrella business and public identity.

### Baddie Walkies

The core paid walking service.

### The Wag List

The working name for recurring clients, priority scheduling, and future loyalty benefits. Do not promise discounts or guaranteed availability until the economics and capacity are defined.

### Hawt Dog Paparazzi

The playful public name for post-walk photography and social content. Private client updates should still be labeled clearly as a **Walk Report**.

### Baddie Charms

Optional leash or bag charms awarded for milestones and loyalty. Avoid collar accessories that could snag or become choking hazards. Do not build retail inventory before demand is demonstrated.

## Target customer

Busy dog owners in Westgate, Paperbox, Zephyr, and nearby Downtown SLC residences who value convenience, personality, discretion, reliable communication, and attentive care.

The brand may be funny; the service must feel conspicuously responsible.

## Positioning

**Primary descriptor:** Downtown dog walking for people with places to be.

**Primary campaign line:** Don’t have that dog in you today? Your dog still does. We’ll take it from here.

**Premium-conversion line:** Pampered pooches. Properly exercised.

## Voice

- Witty, socially observant, and locally aware
- Confident without pretending to be exclusive or luxurious
- Warm toward dogs and nonjudgmental toward owners
- Clear and serious whenever safety, access, scheduling, or money is involved
- Never use humor to minimize a dog’s fear, aggression, illness, injury, or an owner’s privacy

## Usable campaign lines

- Serving looks. Taking walks. Wagging tails.
- Ruff weekend? We’ll take the morning shift.
- Your dog called. They need to get out.
- Pampered pooches. Properly exercised.
- Swiped right and got somewhere to be? We’ve got the walk.
- Your dog has places to sniff and people to see.

## Naming risk to monitor

A local company already uses **Part of the Pack SLC**. The Baddie Pack must remain visually and verbally distinct. Complete Utah business-name and federal trademark checks before major spending.
