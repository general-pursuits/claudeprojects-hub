# Client Journey

## 1. Discovery

Customer finds The Baddie Pack through a resident referral, building channel, social account, QR code, local search, or website.

## 2. Fit request

Customer submits:

- Name and contact information
- Building or general service location
- Dog name, age, approximate size, and breed or mix if known
- Requested service and frequency
- Typical availability needs
- Known reactivity, bite history, escape behavior, medical concerns, or mobility needs

This is a screening request, not an automatic booking.

## 3. Meet and greet

Required before unsupervised access or recurring service. Confirm:

- Dog–walker fit
- Leash, harness, doors, elevators, and building route
- Access method
- Emergency contact and veterinarian
- Feeding, water, cleanup, and home boundaries
- Photo and social-sharing permissions
- Behavior triggers and handling instructions

## 4. Approval and setup

Before the first service, obtain completed intake information, signed service terms, emergency authorization, payment method, access instructions, and explicit photo preferences.

## 5. Booking

Client selects only an approved service. Booking confirmation must state the service window, price, cancellation terms, and access expectation.

## 6. Service

Follow the operating and safety rules. Do not improvise material changes without client approval unless immediate safety requires it.

## 7. Walk Report

Send arrival/departure status, bathroom notes, relevant behavior or health observations, and photos. Keep the tone fun only when nothing safety-related needs clear language.

## 8. Follow-up

After the first service, confirm satisfaction and correct any intake details. Invite recurring clients to The Wag List only when capacity exists.

## 9. Retention and referrals

Use reliable service, recognizable neighborhood presence, milestone charms, and thoughtful reports. Never pressure clients to post publicly or reveal their residence.
