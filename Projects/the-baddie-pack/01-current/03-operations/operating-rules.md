# Operating Rules

## Scheduling

- Publish defined service windows; do not imply 24/7 availability.
- Maintain travel and handoff buffers between bookings.
- Do not accept overlapping commitments that depend on everything going perfectly.
- Confirm recurring schedules in writing.
- Define late-cancellation, lockout, and holiday policies before accepting payment.

## Service delivery

- Use the client-approved leash and properly fitted harness or collar.
- Never walk a dog off leash.
- Do not introduce dogs or enter dog parks without explicit authorization and a documented policy.
- Follow the agreed route boundaries and duration while adapting for immediate safety.
- Do not bring guests, friends, children, or unapproved animals into a client’s home or service.
- Do not smoke, vape, consume alcohol, or use impairing substances before or during service.
- Keep personal phone use limited to navigation, client communication, documentation, and emergencies.

## Communication

- Communicate delays before the service window is missed.
- Report injuries, escapes, bites, access problems, property damage, illness signs, or unusual behavior immediately.
- Separate jokes from operational facts in every Walk Report.
- Never diagnose a health or behavioral condition.

## Capacity

- Launch with one dog at a time unless multiple dogs belong to the same household and have been assessed together.
- Set a conservative daily booking cap after timing the full door-to-door workflow.
- Pause new recurring clients before reliability declines.

## Money

- Publish prices and material fees before booking.
- Keep business income and expenses separate from personal finances as soon as practical.
- Record every payment, refund, business purchase, and mileage expense.
- Do not invent discounts case by case; document any introductory offer and expiration.

## Quality standard

The customer should consistently receive a safe dog, a secured home, an accurate update, and the service they purchased. Brand extras never compensate for failure on those basics.
