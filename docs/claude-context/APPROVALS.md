# APPROVALS - what needs sign-off, and how it flows

Addie approves; sessions draft and pause. The goal is zero accidental public/irreversible actions AND not
drowning her in questions. Batch, default the trivial, interrupt only for real forks.

## Hard gates (always need Addie's explicit yes)
- Anything public: site deploy, directory/listing submit, social post, press, published content.
- Anything irreversible or external: form submissions, OAuth grants, accepting terms, sending email, signing.
- Money: any paid tool, subscription, or spend (default is always the free route).
- Client-facing sends: proposals, SOWs, invoices, any deliverable leaving to a client.
- Deleting or overwriting a master; destructive Notion ops.

## Auto-OK (no approval needed; still logged with a manifest line)
Drafts in working-drafts/ or the hub; internal notes and research; mockups; task capture; reference-doc
edits; anything reversible that stays inside the hub.

## How to request approval (respect her attention)
- Batch decisions and present them together, not one at a time. Use AskUserQuestion with concrete options and
  a recommendation, never open-ended "what do you want?".
- Show the mockup (mobile + web) for anything visual. State whether each choice is reversible, and the default
  you will take if she does not answer.
- Never block a whole task on a trivial choice you can default. State the assumption and proceed.
- While waiting on her, keep other independent work moving (approval gates its own branch, not the whole job).

## Requirements before you ask (so a yes is safe to act on)
Output pipeline run (voice-scrub, named-entity-check, verify-work); holds + proof-bank checked; DoD met;
sources cited; the change is scoped and reversible where possible.

## Record every approval
Log what was approved, the date, and the exact deploy/commit/send it authorized. One deploy per approval
round. Each granted decision also becomes a DECISIONS.md line so it is never re-asked.
