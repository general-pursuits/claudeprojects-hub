# TASK TRACKING - one system so nothing gets forgotten

Goal: every to-do, next action, commitment, and blocker lives in ONE place, gets a due date, and gets
surfaced back to Addie on a schedule so she is not the one who has to remember.

## The one master

- Master board: the Notion Tasks database inside "master project tracker- addie"
  (https://app.notion.com/p/3b1166342f47804cb870d1354dd3613a). Use Tasks (not Task Board) for the working list.
- SEO / marketing items also live in the "Marketing & SEO To-Do List"
  (https://app.notion.com/p/3af166342f4781c7943cf48191e9b1c4) -> when working SEO, update BOTH is being
  retired: treat the Notion Tasks board as master and log SEO items there too, tagged SEO.
- Task statuses (canonical): Backlog / On Deck / In Progress / In Review / On Hold / Done / Archived.

## Capture rule (the important one)

Any next action, promise, or blocker that surfaces in ANY chat gets written to the Tasks board immediately,
before the chat moves on. Fields: what, Status, Due date (if known), Priority, related Project/Client,
and a one-line context. A to-do mentioned but not logged does not exist. Never rely on memory or chat scrollback.

## What keeps Addie tasked (automations)

- Daily task brief (weekday morning MT): overdue first, then due today, then top On Deck / In Progress.
  One short push. Quiet if there is nothing pressing.
- Weekly review (Friday afternoon MT): what moved, what stalled (On Hold / no movement in 7 days),
  unfiled meeting recaps, and the 3 things to focus on next week. Proposes; does not change tasks without a reply.
- Blockers roll into the Danger/blockers view and get nagged until cleared (GBP re-verify, Bing postcard,
  business phone, Cal.com addie@ verify).

## Rules

- One master, no parallel task lists. Other trackers become linked views or pointers, never siblings.
- Use check-open-items to scan for anything open before a review.
- Recurring/again-later items get a Due date and On Hold, never dropped.
- Close the loop: when a deliverable ships, its follow-up becomes a new Task; the finished one goes Done.
