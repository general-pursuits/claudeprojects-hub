# Opportunity Designed repurposing

One piece of thinking should do five jobs. Take an existing asset and produce the derived formats without diluting it.

## Source selection

Prefer, in order: a published field note in the live site's field-notes folder, an approved draft, a closeout document from a finished engagement, or a specific idea Addie types.

Read the actual source before writing. Do not summarize from the title.

## Formats to produce

Generate all four unless Addie names a subset.

**LinkedIn text post (founder profile, /in/addiemorrow/)**
- Open with the claim, not a windup. First line has to survive the truncation fold.
- One idea. 120 to 200 words.
- Written from experience, first person, specific to a category or a retailer type.
- No hashtag stacks. Two at most, or none.
- Close with a question or a flat statement. No "link in comments" theater unless the link genuinely matters.

**LinkedIn carousel**
- 6 to 8 slides. One sentence per slide, display type in Anton.
- Slide 1 is the hook, the last slide is the takeaway plus the wordmark.
- Charcoal or parchment grounds, violet accent. Mobile-first sizing.
- Deliver a visual mockup for approval before any export.

**Company page variant**
Same idea, company voice rather than first person. Shorter. This is the hello@ tone, not the addie@ tone.

**Newsletter blurb**
Three to five sentences pointing to the full field note, with the article link and the standard footer subscribe framing.

## Handoff

- Present the drafts as batched options where a line is a judgment call, rather than revising one line at a time.
- Nothing posts automatically. Deliver the copy and any graphics for Addie to publish.
- Every derived piece links back to the canonical page on opportunitydesigned.com so the entity signal compounds.

## Guardrails

Read `references/guardrails.md` before producing anything. It carries the voice rules, confidentiality rules, pricing rules, channel rules, and brand constants, and it names which standalone skill owns each check.

Before output: run `named-entity-check` on anything naming a company or person, and `voice-scrub` on the prose. Do not reimplement either check here.
