# Opportunity Designed delivery kit

Produce consistent engagement artifacts so delivery is fast and the quality does not depend on how the week went.

## The Opportunity Blueprint

Three phases. Copy is locked in the framework threading pack. Do not restate a phase in new words, and do not attach a fixed deliverable list to a phase in any client-facing method copy.

- **Map** — find the unrealized opportunity. Where the business is leaving distribution, assortment, or positioning on the table.
- **Design** — decide what to do about it. Priorities, sequence, tradeoffs.
- **Build** — make it real. The assets, the pitch, the plan someone can execute Monday.

## Standard artifacts

Pick what the signed scope calls for. Do not invent extra deliverables.

**Kickoff pack**
Scope restated, decisions needed and by when, data and access requests, milestone dates, single point of contact, what the client will see and when.

**Opportunity Map**
Current-state read, category and channel landscape, where the brand actually sits versus where it thinks it sits, ranked list of unrealized opportunities with the reasoning visible, and the three that matter most.

**Design readout**
The recommendation, the sequence, what to stop doing, the tradeoffs stated openly, and the first ninety days.

**Build assets**
Whatever the scope named: retailer pitch narrative, assortment architecture, line plan logic, channel strategy, positioning language, meeting-ready materials.

**Closeout**
What was delivered, what changed, what to watch, what would be worth doing next. This document is the source for a later case study.

## Production standards

- Every visual gets a reviewable mockup before it is final, shown in both mobile and web views. Design mobile-first.
- Brand palette: charcoal #2F2D2A and parchment #ECE7DD as grounds, ink #EDEAE3 and #1A1714, violet #8E72C6 with hover #6B4FA6, lavender #B49CE0, gray #6B655B. Type is Anton for display and Inter for text.
- Icons follow the basic set in purple and gray. No emojis anywhere in client-facing work.
- The word "playbook" is banned. Use the actual name of the thing.
- Deck work goes through the pptx skill, documents through docx, spreadsheets through xlsx.

## Filing

Drafts live in the working-drafts folder for the engagement. Nothing moves to a client-shared location without Addie's approval. Save reference material locally on the Mac and mirror to Notion, treating the local file as the source of truth.

## Guardrails

Read `references/guardrails.md` before producing anything. It carries the voice rules, confidentiality rules, pricing rules, channel rules, and brand constants, and it names which standalone skill owns each check.

Before output: run `named-entity-check` on anything naming a company or person, and `voice-scrub` on the prose. Do not reimplement either check here.
